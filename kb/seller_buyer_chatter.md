## Themes

**Seller frustration is about notification, not the policy.** The most common complaint across the sample is not that refunds are faster, but that sellers were not told which returns would behave this way and could not see it on the order page. Several posts explicitly say the feature itself is reasonable.

**Cash flow is the concrete harm.** Sellers describe being debited while the item is in transit. Lower-volume sellers and those with thin margins report this most acutely. Some responded by restricting returns or raising prices.

**Buyer sentiment is positive.** Every buyer-side post about the fast refund is favourable. Buyers who did not receive it were confused about why, but not unhappy.

**Support readiness was a gap.** Multiple sellers report that frontline agents did not initially know about the Return Discrepancy process and routed them incorrectly first. This appears repeatedly from early July onward.

**Geography.** No posts in the sample report this behaviour outside the US.

**Trajectory.** Volume of discussion is low through May and early June, rises sharply in the first two weeks of July, and stays elevated through early August. Sentiment is negative on the seller side and positive on the buyer side throughout, with the negative side growing louder over time.
